VISHAL TRADERS is a retail agricultural equipment shop located in Lugharwara, Seoni, Madhya Pradesh.

The shop sells agricultural equipment including cultivators, trolleys, rotavators and seedrills.

Customer records use name, mobile number and address. Customer GSTIN is not required for the basic customer record.

The shop wants automatic stock reduction after sales. Cancelled bills should be voided rather than permanently deleted.

Supported payment methods include Cash, UPI, Bank, Credit and Cheque.
